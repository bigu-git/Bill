🃏 NOW OR NEVER

One card can change everything.

NOW OR NEVER is a multiplayer party card game designed for friends, couples, and groups. Draw a card, reveal a question or challenge, and see what happens next.

Simple rules. Unexpected questions. Unforgettable moments.

---

✨ Features

- 6 Unique Categories – Different colors, questions, and challenges.
- Multiplayer – Play with 2–12 players on one shared device.
- Interactive Card Flip – Smooth 3D card-flipping animations.
- Custom Questions – Create, edit, and delete your own questions.
- Random Card Draw – Let the game decide what comes next.
- Player Rotation – Automatic turn management.
- Question History – Track previously drawn cards.
- Custom Settings – Enable or disable categories and adjust game rules.
- Local Storage – Save custom questions and settings automatically.
- Mobile Friendly – Designed for phones, tablets, and desktops.

🎴 Card Categories

Color| Category| Description
🔴 Red| SPICE| Bold and spicy questions
⚫ Black| DRINK| Drinking-themed questions and alternatives
⚪ White| FREE PASS| Skip, escape, or special privileges
🟡 Gold| TRUTH| Honest and personal questions
🔵 Blue| CHALLENGE| Fun and creative challenges
🟣 Purple| CHAOS| Random events involving everyone

🎮 How to Play

1. Open the game.
2. Add at least 2 players.
3. Select your preferred categories.
4. Start the game.
5. Take turns drawing cards.
6. Flip the card to reveal its question or challenge.
7. Complete the card or use an available skip option.
8. Pass the turn to the next player.

No complicated rules. Just draw, flip, and play.

💾 Data & Privacy

- Custom questions are stored locally in your browser.
- Player settings are saved using LocalStorage.
- No account or registration is required.
- No personal information is sent to a server.

Clearing browser storage may erase saved custom questions and settings.
