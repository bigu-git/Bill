            How to play

            1. Gather your group. Add 2 to 12 players. Everyone shares one device.
            2. Draw a card. The current player taps Draw card. The card flips and reveals a question or challenge.
            3. Play it out. Answer, perform, or follow the card. Tap Done when finished, or Skip if you pass.
            4. Pass the phone. The turn moves to the next player automatically.
            5. Make it yours. Add your own questions in Create questions. They appear in the game right away.

            The six categories

            Spice
            Bold, spicy, relationship-related questions.
            Drink
            Drinking challenges. Always optional: water, a skip, or an honest answer works too.
            Free pass
            Skip, escape, or change the game.
            Truth
            Deep, personal, honest questions.
            Challenge
            Funny and creative dares.
            Chaos
            Random events that involve everyone.

            Play responsibly. Nobody should be pressured to answer, perform, or drink. Skipping is always allowed.
